Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Rebalance Point Massage handles information voluntarily shared through scheduling and communication workflows.

Effective date: March 12, 2026

Information We Collect

When you choose to communicate with us, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, and communication details related to scheduling, intake, or appointment coordination.

How We Use Information

We use communication details to support care operations, including appointment reminders, scheduling updates, customer support responses, and other administrative communications related to your services.

Mobile SMS Messaging Privacy Policy

By providing your mobile number through booking, intake, or direct communication workflows, you agree to receive informational text messages from Rebalance Point Massage, including appointment reminders and scheduling updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messaging. Reply HELP for assistance.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This includes text messaging opt-in data and consent.

Scheduling Platform Note

Online scheduling is handled through Carepatron's secure platform, where applicable. Rebalance Point Massage does not use a direct contact form on this website.
