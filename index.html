About Our Clinic

A local small business dedicated to your holistic health journey

Meet Patrick & Jodie Stewart

Patrick and Jodie Stewart are licensed massage therapists who met while studying massage therapy at Carrington College before beginning to practice professionally in 2019. Together they founded Rebalance Point Massage with a shared commitment to providing thoughtful, evidence-informed therapeutic bodywork.

Their work is grounded in a science-based approach to massage therapy and draws from techniques such as Muscle Energy Techniques (MET), myofascial therapy, trigger point therapy, and lymphatic drainage to address pain, improve mobility, and support the body's natural recovery processes.

Their sessions are often highly customized to each client's short- and long-term goals, addressing specific musculoskeletal complaints and functional restrictions while taking into account the broader structural and compensatory patterns that may be contributing to them.

Their clinical experience includes working with acute musculoskeletal issues and chronic pain patterns, particularly involving the spine, shoulders, and pelvic complex, helping clients restore mobility, reduce discomfort, and improve functional movement.

At their Spokane clinic, Patrick and Jodie aim to provide a professional and welcoming environment where clients can receive individualized therapeutic care tailored to their needs and long-term wellbeing.

Rebalance Point Workplace Reset

We bring brief, fully clothed chair massage sessions directly to Spokane-area workplaces and events. Patrick and Jodie adapt each 10–12 minute session to the individual, focusing on areas such as the neck, shoulders, upper back, forearms, and hands.

Suitable for employee and staff appreciation, wellness days, team events, and recurring workplace programs.

  • Approximately 5–6 participants per therapist-hour
  • One or two licensed massage therapists, depending on group size
  • Massage chairs, setup, standard treatment supplies, and sanitation materials provided

Sporting Events
